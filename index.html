Good to know

Questions, answered

How fast will my order arrive?

Orders ship within 24 hours and typically arrive in 2–5 working days across India. Shipping is free over ₹999, otherwise ₹80.

How should I store my tea?

Keep the tin sealed, away from light, heat and strong smells. Properly stored, our teas stay at their best for 12 months.

What’s the difference between first and second flush?

First flush is plucked in spring and is light, floral and bright. Second flush, picked in early summer, is fuller with the famous muscatel sweetness.

Can I return an opened tin?

Yes — if you’re not happy, tell us within 14 days and we’ll refund or replace it. No fuss.
